# Fixed Quarterly Audit Checklist

This is the standard checklist used every quarter, for every client. When the user pastes their completed checklist, compare it against this list and flag any items that are missing from what was pasted (don't block on it — just note the gap).

## GBP Checklist (feeds: GBP Optimization category)
1. Is Business Name correct?
2. Does Address Match Workbook and Website?
3. Business Primary Category is?
4. Are there any recent reviews in last few weeks?
5. Client Responding to Reviews?
6. Recent Photos in past few weeks?
7. Google Posts in last week?
8. Description in Listing?
9. Website URL Going to Homepage or Location Page correctly?
10. Tracking Number on listing with additional phone as main number matching Website/Workbook?
11. Profile strength 100%?
12. Relevant searches in searches breakdown?
13. Interactions increase (where appropriate)?
14. Gain on Competitors in Reviews?

## Website Checklist (feeds: Website Foundation & On-Page SEO category)
1. Website displaying correctly on desktop and mobile?
2. Form displaying correctly?
3. Brand Name on Website/Title Tags match GBP?
4. Address on site match GBP?
5. Phone on site match GBP?
6. Service area pages linked correctly from Homepage/Service Area page?
7. Recent Blog posts displaying for the month?
8. Check Title Tags for home/location page - is correct City/Service Area Keyword in there?
9. Local Schema Correct?
10. GA is working fine?
11. Website Speed within Range?

## Citations/Brand Entity Checklist (feeds: Local SEO & Brand Visibility categories)
1. Citations NAP match website & GBP?
2. Brand Search + Main City/Service Area - Client Brand mentions populating first 20 results?
3. Check Brand Searches (Last 28 days)

## GSC/Indexing Checklist (feeds: Brand Visibility & Website Foundation categories)
1. Sitemap submitted and crawled recently?
2. Upwards Trend in Impressions? (Brand Name)
3. Upwards Trend in Clicks? (Brand Name)
4. Recent Blog Posts Indexed?
5. Non-Indexed City Pages?
6. Homepage Upwards Impressions Trend (non-branded traffic)?
7. Homepage Upwards Clicks Trend (non-branded traffic)?
8. City Pages Upwards Movement since last check?

## Ahrefs/Heatmap Checklist (feeds: Off-Page Authority & Local SEO categories)
1. Lifetime Increase in Overall Rankings?
2. Main City KWs in 1st Page of Results?
3. Referring Domains Upward Trend?
4. DR (Domain Rating) Increase?

## Ranking Data Convention
If the user shares Local Dominator (or similar rank tracker) data alongside the checklist: **lower position number = better ranking.** A keyword moving from 4.77 to 4.46 is an improvement. A keyword moving from 3.41 to 3.65 is a decline. Never assume higher = better for this data.
