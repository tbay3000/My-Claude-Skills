# SEO Scoring Rubric

Total Score: **0–100**, split across five categories, each scored **0–20**.

## Category 1: Website Foundation & On-Page SEO (0–20)
Technical site health, content coverage, user experience, core keyword alignment.

- **0–5 Poor**: Very thin site (fewer than 5 pages); missing key service pages or outdated/duplicate content; broken links, slow load speed, not mobile-friendly; no schema; poorly optimized title tags.
- **6–10 Basic**: Core pages exist but lack supporting content (service areas, blog); limited keyword targeting; SEO fundamentals implemented inconsistently; outdated navigation/design.
- **11–15 Good**: Most core and supporting pages built; clean internal linking and metadata; mobile-friendly with solid UX; early blog/content strategy underway.
- **16–20 Excellent**: Fully built out with rich service and location pages; content mapped to buyer intent; strong technical SEO (speed, schema, mobile); consistent blog and internal linking.

## Category 2: GBP Optimization (0–20)
Setup, optimization, and activity level of Google Business Profile.

- **0–5 Poor**: Not claimed/verified; missing essential info; no description or images; fewer than 10 reviews; no keyword optimization; no posting in 3+ months.
- **6–10 Basic**: Claimed and verified; core info filled; under 25 reviews and/or <4.0 average; minimal keyword optimization; fewer than 1 new review/month; infrequent updates (every 2-3 months).
- **11–15 Good**: All info accurate; 25-75 reviews at 4.2+ average; description includes key services/keywords; categories/services complete; monthly updates; actively responding to reviews; GBP connected to website with schema.
- **16–20 Excellent**: 100% filled and maintained; 75+ reviews with consistent frequency (2-5/month); 4.5+ average with responses; weekly posting/offers; high-quality photos regularly; services/categories fully optimized; Q&A monitored; top 3 for core local terms.

## Category 3: Local SEO & Map Pack Presence (0–20)
Map visibility, NAP consistency, citations, local relevance.

- **0–5 Poor**: Inconsistent/missing directory listings; barely ranks in local maps; NAP errors in key directories.
- **6–10 Basic**: Shows up for branded/niche keywords only; basic citation presence (10-20 sites), not fully consistent; no localized content or city pages.
- **11–15 Good**: Map pack visibility in some service areas; clean NAP across top 50+ citations; local content strategy started (service area pages).
- **16–20 Excellent**: Consistently top 3 in map pack for core terms; full citation consistency (top 100+); strong presence in local news, community pages, local backlinks.

## Category 4: Off-Page Authority — Links & Referring Domains (0–20)
- **0–5 Poor**: Fewer than 10 referring domains; DR under 5; no link-building.
- **6–10 Basic**: 10-30 referring domains; DR 5-15; a few basic directory/PR links.
- **11–15 Good**: 30-75 referring domains; DR 15-30; mix of niche, guest post, and branded links.
- **16–20 Excellent**: 75+ high-quality referring domains; DR over 30; consistent link-building (citations, PR, guest posts).

## Category 5: Brand Visibility & Indexation (0–20)
How visible the brand is across Google and third-party platforms.

- **0–5 Poor**: Fewer than 10 branded search results; no branded traffic in GSC; little/no third-party or social presence.
- **6–10 Basic**: 10-30 branded search results; minimal brand searches/impressions in GSC; some directory/local site listings.
- **11–15 Good**: 30-75 branded mentions/indexed pages; branded traffic increasing steadily; featured on multiple third-party platforms/articles.
- **16–20 Excellent**: 75+ indexed results tied to brand; branded traffic is a major share of total GSC impressions; brand widely mentioned and searched.

## Health Status Bands (applies to TOTAL score, 0-100)
- **0–29**: 🔥 Critical
- **30–59**: 🔴 Red
- **60–79**: 🟡 Yellow
- **80–100**: 🟢 Green
